overwritten from this repository.

## What S4 is (as built)

A platform for UK private security companies to manage Guards, clients, sites, rotas and shifts; record
attendance (with optional GPS verification at Book On / Book Off), Welfare Checks, Log Book entries,
incidents, Site Requests and emergency alerts; run timesheets, payroll and invoicing preparation; and manage
compliance and screening workflows. Clients of a security company may use a client portal.

## What S4 is NOT — the terms must not claim otherwise

- **Not certified lone-worker monitoring.** S4 records Welfare Checks and raises alerts to the security
  company's own control room. It does not provide an alarm receiving centre, BS 8484 service, or 24/7
  monitoring by S4.
- **No guaranteed emergency response.** An emergency alert in S4 notifies the company; S4 does not dispatch
  help and does not contact emergency services. Users must call 999 in an emergency.
- **No guarantee of delivery or timing** for alerts, notifications or emails (they depend on devices,
  networks and third-party services).
- **Not a screening/vetting guarantee.** Company-managed deployment is the company's own responsibility
  (the in-product statement: "This company manages its own employment, screening and deployment compliance
  for company-managed guards unless S4 separately provides a specified screening service.").
- **Not legal, HR, payroll or tax advice.** Pay and invoice figures are calculated from the company's own
  rules and approvals.
- **Not local-first.** Records are stored on S4's servers.

## Accounts

- Self-registration is available for Guards and company owners; a new account must verify its email
  address before it can sign in. Company staff and client-portal users are created by their company.
- Users must keep credentials secure; S4 offers password reset by emailed one-time link (30 minutes).
- S4 may suspend accounts; a suspended or deleted account cannot sign in or use the API.

## Account deletion

As described in the Privacy drafting pack §7: access is removed and direct identifiers are deleted or
anonymised; operational and security records may be retained for legal, contractual, regulatory, safety
or evidential purposes. Company owners must contact support (workspace transfer or closure).

## Acceptable use (to draft)

No false records; no misuse of another person's account; no attempts to access another company's data;
no interference with the service; Guards must only Book On where they actually are.

## Company responsibilities (to draft)

Accuracy of schedules, pay rules and approvals; lawful use of Guard data; their own deployment, licensing
(SIA) and right-to-work compliance; responding to their own alerts.
