# Company verification: S4 administrator checklist

Use this when deciding a company in **Platform Admin → Company verification**.

## What S4 approval means, and what it does not

- **S4 approval** is S4's own platform decision. It confirms that the company is real, that it is who it says it is, and that S4 accepts it as a customer. Only then can it publish jobs, invite Guards and create shifts on S4.
- **S4 approval is not SIA accreditation.**
  - It does not mean the company holds SIA Approved Contractor Scheme (ACS) status.
  - It does not confirm any individual's SIA licence. Every Guard's licence and right to work is still checked separately, by the employing company, before each assignment.
- Never describe an approved company as "SIA approved", "accredited" or "vetted by S4" unless that has been checked separately.
- The app does not record ACS status. Approval does not depend on it unless S4 policy changes.

## Before approving a pending company

1. **Name and number:** look up the company number on the Companies House register. Check that:
   - the registered name matches;
   - the company is **active** (not dissolved or in liquidation).
2. **Address:** the registered address is plausible and consistent with Companies House.
3. **Contact:** the owner email and contact details are reachable and belong to the company. Do not rely on a free webmail address alone for an unknown company: make contact.
4. **Duplicates:** search the list for the same number, name or owner email. A second registration of an existing customer needs an explanation.
5. **Industry:** the company provides security services, or has a clear reason to employ Guards through S4.
6. **Records:** note anything unusual in the reason or in your own records, then decide.

## Deciding

- **Approve:** all checks pass. No reason is needed.
- **Reject:** the details do not check out. The **reason is mandatory and the company sees it**, so be specific and actionable. For example: "Company number 1234 5678 is registered to a different name at Companies House. Correct your registered name or number, then ask S4 to reconsider."
- **Suspend** (approved companies only): use this for a serious concern about a company already operating, such as lapsed insurance, a complaint under investigation, or non-payment.
  - The **reason is mandatory and the company sees it**.
  - Suspension stops new work only. Safety alerts, incidents and work already in place continue, so Guards stay safe and are paid.
- **Reinstate:** the concern behind a suspension is resolved.
- Every decision asks for confirmation and is recorded in the company's verification history and the audit log.
- If S4 reports that the company "changed while you were deciding", another administrator has acted. Read the history before deciding again.

## Reasons: do and don't

- **Do:** state the fact, what is wrong, and what the company can do next.
- **Don't:** include personal data about individuals, internal notes, suspicions you cannot share, or another company's details. The reason is shown to everyone in the company who can see its verification status.

## Reconsideration requests

- A rejected company can correct its details and ask S4 to reconsider. It then returns to **Pending**, and the history shows its message.
- Repeat the checks above on the corrected details; do not just re-approve.

## Boundaries

- Never approve a company because it asked by email or phone without doing the checks.
- Never approve a test company in production unless that has been agreed for a specific test. Suspend it when the test ends.
- S4 approval is decided only in the admin page or the documented admin API. There is no other route.
